- Fixed the cursor jumping to the end of the text box while typing in composers — most visible when quoting a note, where typed text could land after the quoted link
- Android keyboard autocorrect and autocomplete no longer garble composer text or insert at the wrong spot
- Emoji, GIF, media and poll inserts now leave the cursor right after the inserted content on every platform
- Overhauled @-mention autocomplete behind one shared engine, now shared by the composer, search bars, mention dialog, code user picker, calendar participants and pack members
  - Matches NIP-05 addresses and npub/hex pubkeys, not just display names
  - Relay search results stream in live as they arrive and are remembered in your profile cache
  - Paste a user@domain.com address to resolve a NIP-05 identity, or a full npub/nprofile to jump straight to that person
  - A bare @ now suggests thread participants, people you recently mentioned, and accounts you follow
- Mentions in the composer insert as pretty @Name labels and become proper nostr: profile links when you publish; the links carry each user's actual relay hints from the outbox map and their nostr.json document
- Notes you publish now also go to the known relays of everyone you mention, so mentions actually reach them
- Quoting a note no longer overwrites the draft of your regular home composer
- Community and topic composers now support @-mention autocomplete
- The mention dropdown dismisses when you click away, and no longer steals focus while choosing a suggestion
- Search overhaul:
  - Search operators now actually work: kind:, from:, since:, until:, #tag, -exclude, lang: and domain: are parsed into real filters instead of being full-text-searched as one blob
  - #hashtag searches filter by tag on all relays (no search-relay dependency) with instant local matches first
  - Typing or pasting an npub, note, event, or address opens the right viewer directly instead of searching the string
  - Results stream in progressively from search relays instead of one blocking fetch, with local database matches shown instantly
  - A clear banner appears when no NIP-50 search relays are reachable, instead of silently returning nothing
  - Search relays connect in parallel with a ~6s bound (was up to 30s sequentially on first search), expanded from 2 to 5 defaults, with NIP-11 capability checks that exclude relays which definitively lack NIP-50
  - Switching tabs no longer refetches — recent results are cached and refreshed in the background
  - "Relevance" sort option, "Load more" pagination (50→150), and per-item delete buttons in search history
  - from:name resolves against your cached profiles, with an "unknown author" hint when no match is found
  - Trending source defaults unified between the sidebar and the /trending page
